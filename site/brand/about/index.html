Founded on 10 November 2016, Yunnan Nahan Agriculture Technology Co., Ltd. is based in Nahan Village, Dajie Township, Jingdong, Pu'er, Yunnan. We tend our own tea garden and hold full food-compliance production qualifications.

Our house brand, Dazhi, covers the whole chain — from garden care and fresh-leaf picking to first processing and brand records. To us, good tea is not a packaged story; it comes from clean ecology, steady craft and long-term consistency.

This site is not a single product page. It is an open tea-culture journal. Through professional, honest and restrained writing, we help more people understand Yunnan tea — and let the ecological tea of the Nahan mountains become known.
