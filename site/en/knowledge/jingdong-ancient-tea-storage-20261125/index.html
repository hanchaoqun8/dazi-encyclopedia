The Everyday Art of Storing Ancient Tree Tea

In the countryside of Jingdong, storing ancient tree tea isn't a mysterious ritual but part of daily life. Farmers use simple methods to let time

In the early morning, farmer Li opens the tea storage, letting air circulate as sunlight filters through bamboo curtains. 'Tea leaves need to breathe, just like us,' he says. Ancient tree tea rests in ceramic jars with slightly loose lids, allowing gentle exposure to air and light. Every few days, he gently turns the leaves, feeling their subtle changes. Tea is a daily beverage, not a substitute for medicine. These simple actions represent generations of wisdom, helping the tea gradually develop its rich aroma and flavor over time.
