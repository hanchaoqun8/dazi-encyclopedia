Everyday Wisdom of Ancient Tea Tree Storage

From warehouse to cup: storage wisdom for ancient tea. Tea is a daily beverage, not medicine. Compiled from Dazhi field notes in Nahang Village

Opening the tea warehouse in the morning, the air carries the rich aroma of aged Pu'er. Storing ancient tree tea doesn't require complex rituals—just a dry, ventilated spot away from strong odors. Over time, the tea cakes transform slowly, much like the fine lines that mark a tea lover's face. When brewing, water temperature and pouring height all influence the final taste. Tea is a daily beverage, not a substitute for medicine. Remember, each leaf carries the breath of the mountains and the sweat of the tea farmers.
