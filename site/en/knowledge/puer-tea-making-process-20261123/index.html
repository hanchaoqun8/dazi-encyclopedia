The Journey of Puer Tea: From Leaf to Cup

Discover the steps of Puer tea making, from leaf selection to pressing, and how everyday tea is crafted. Compiled from Dazhi field notes in Nahang

The journey begins with careful leaf plucking in early morning. Puer tea undergoes withering, fixation, rolling, and sun-drying. Traditional artisans rely on experience to monitor fermentation, allowing time to transform the tea's character. Finally, the leaves are pressed into cakes. Tea is a daily beverage, not a substitute for medicine. When brewing, watch the leaves unfurl in hot water and savor the mellow aroma that develops through this careful process.
