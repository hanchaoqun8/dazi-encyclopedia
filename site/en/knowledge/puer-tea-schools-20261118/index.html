Puer Tea: Schools and Arsenal

Puer tea has various schools, from raw to ripe, ancient trees to mountain origins—each a unique tool in a tea lover's arsenal.

Brewing a cake of raw puer in the morning yields a clear liquor with a slight astringency that gives way to a lasting sweetness—raw puer's signature style, sharp yet nuanced. Ripe puer, fermented through wet piling, offers a rich, mellow brew like a master's rounded technique. Ancient tree teas, grown in remote forests for centuries, boast complex flavors, prized as treasures by enthusiasts. Terroir-driven mountain teas each have distinct characters: Bulang's boldness, Yiwu's gentleness. Puer tea is a daily beverage, not a substitute for medicine.
