Dazhi holds three commitments:

  1. Traceable origin — our own garden and first-processing chain, clear and traceable.
  2. Ecology first — respecting the forest environment of Nahan and staying on the ecological-tea path.
  3. Restrained craft — no artificial ageing, no forced aroma; the leaf's true character is kept.

Good tea does not need heavy modification. Clean raw material and moderate craft already express the origin.
