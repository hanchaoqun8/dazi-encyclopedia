Our garden sits in Nahan Village, Dajie Township, Jingdong, Yunnan — a landscape of encircling forest where tea trees grow alongside mountain ecology. We manage the garden ecologically, respecting the natural rhythm of the trees and limiting unnecessary intervention, so the leaf expresses the place it comes from.

Mist, moderate sun and day–night temperature differences slow growth and build layers of aroma and body. This is the terroir behind every Dazhi tea.
