Puer Ancient Tea Tree Tea with Temperature Fluctuations

Compiled from Dazhi field notes in Nahang Village, Jingdong, with restrained, practical detail for everyday reading.Compiled from Dazhi field notes in

Puer ancient tea tree tea is highly sensitive to temperature changes during storage. High temperatures accelerate aging, while low temperatures may affect its transformation process. The ideal storage environment should maintain constant temperature, ventilation, and protection from light. Tea is a daily beverage, not a substitute for medicine. Proper storage not only preserves the original flavor but also allows the tea to gradually transform under suitable conditions, revealing richer layers of complexity.
