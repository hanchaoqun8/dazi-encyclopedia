## Overview
Nahang (那罕) is a village in Dajie Township, Jingdong County, Yunnan, China.
Located in the heart of Pu-erh tea production territory, this small village has gained recognition for its systematic approach to recording data throughout the tea production process.
The documentation methods employed by Nahang tea producers represent a meticulous approach that preserves information from the moment tea leaves are plucked to their final storage in aging warehouses.
This report examines the comprehensive data recording system implemented in Nahang, Dajie Township, highlighting the methods and boundaries of their documentation practices that contribute to the unique characteristics of Jingdong Dajie Township Pu-erh tea.

## History & Origin
The tradition of tea cultivation in Nahang dates back several generations, with families maintaining records of their harvests and processing methods through handwritten logs and oral histories.
The village's location in Jingdong County provides ideal growing conditions for tea trees, with elevation variations creating microclimates that influence leaf characteristics.
The formalization of data recording methods in Nahang began in the early 2000s when local producers recognized the value of systematic documentation for quality control and consistency.
This evolution from traditional methods to structured record-keeping represents a significant development in the village's tea production history, particularly as the Dazhi brand began incorporating Nahang teas into their collection.

## Craft & Processing
The data recording system in Nahang encompasses every stage of processing.
Garden records include detailed picking batch information, with each harvest assigned a unique identifier that tracks the specific garden section, elevation, and date of collection.
Weather observations are meticulously documented, including temperature, humidity, and rainfall patterns during the picking period.
Drying logs record the duration and conditions of withering and drying processes, while storage checks maintain records of environmental conditions in temporary storage facilities before final processing.
These records create a comprehensive database that producers can reference to understand how different conditions affect the final product, allowing for adjustments in subsequent production cycles.

## Appearance, Aroma & Taste
Nahang's systematic documentation extends to sensory evaluation of the finished tea.
Producers maintain detailed tasting notes that capture the appearance, aroma, and taste profiles of each batch.
These records include observations on the dry leaf color, shape, and texture, as well as the wet leaf characteristics and liquor color.
Aroma notes document fragrance at various stages of brewing, while taste records capture flavor intensity, mouthfeel, and aftertaste.
This sensory data is cross-referenced with processing and storage information to establish correlations between production conditions and final product characteristics, creating a valuable reference for maintaining quality consistency.

## How to Brew
While not directly part of the village's core data recording system, information on brewing methods is maintained as supplementary documentation.
These records include recommended water temperatures, steeping times, and vessel types for different Nahang tea varieties.
The data helps producers understand optimal brewing parameters for their teas and provides guidance for consumers.
This information is particularly valuable for new harvests, as brewing parameters may need adjustment based on the current year's processing conditions and storage duration.

## Benefits & Who It Suits
Daily drinking of Nahang Pu-erh tea offers a unique sensory experience appreciated by tea connoisseurs.
The tea's flavor profile varies depending on the specific harvest and processing methods documented in the village's records.
Regular consumers often develop preferences for particular production cycles or processing styles, which they can identify through the batch numbers maintained by producers.
The systematic approach to data recording ensures that each batch can be traced back to its specific growing and processing conditions, allowing consumers to select teas that match their taste preferences.

## Cautions & Considerations
The data recording system in Nahang has boundaries that producers acknowledge.
While comprehensive, the system cannot account for all variables that may affect tea quality, such as unexpected weather events or subtle differences in processing techniques between individual workers.
Additionally, the records primarily document objective measurements and observable characteristics, while subjective elements of tea appreciation remain outside the formal documentation.
Consumers should understand that while the data provides valuable information, each batch may still exhibit unique characteristics not fully captured in the records.

## Buying, Authentication & Storage
For consumers interested in Nahang teas, the village's data recording system provides tools for authentication.
Each batch carries identifiers that can be cross-referenced with the producer's records to verify origin and processing details.
When purchasing, buyers should request batch information and verify it against the producer's documentation.
Proper storage conditions are also documented in the village's records, including temperature, humidity, and ventilation parameters.
Following these documented storage guidelines helps preserve the tea's characteristics and allows consumers to track how the tea evolves over time.

## Final Thoughts
The systematic approach to data recording in Nahang village represents a sophisticated method of preserving information throughout the tea production cycle.
From garden records tracking picking batches to detailed storage documentation, the village's system creates a comprehensive database that supports quality control and consistency.
While boundaries exist in what can be documented, the system provides valuable insights into how growing conditions and processing methods affect the final product.
For producers and consumers alike, Nahang's data recording methods offer a model for understanding and appreciating the complex factors that contribute to the unique characteristics of Jingdong Dajie Township Pu-erh tea.
